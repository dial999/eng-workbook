\section{Reading Time! / ¡Hora de leer!}

\begin{readingbox}{A Day in the Life of Sofia}
My name is Sofia and I am 11 years old. I live in Guadalajara, Mexico.

On school days, I \textbf{wake up} at 6:30 in the morning. First, I \textbf{take a shower} and \textbf{get dressed}. Then I \textbf{have breakfast} with my family. I usually eat eggs and toast.

At 7:30, I \textbf{go to school} by car. My school starts at 8 o'clock. I love English class! After school, I \textbf{have lunch} at home at 2 p.m.

In the afternoon, I \textbf{do my homework} and then I \textbf{play outside} with my friends. In the evening, I \textbf{watch TV} or \textbf{read a book}.

At 9 p.m. I \textbf{have dinner} with my family, and at 9:30 I \textbf{go to bed}. I love my daily routine!
\end{readingbox}
