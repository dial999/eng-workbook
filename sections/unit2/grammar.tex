\section{Grammar Focus / Enfoque gramatical}

\begin{grammartip}{Simple Present — Affirmative · Presente simple — Afirmativo}
We use the \textbf{Simple Present} to talk about habits, routines, and facts. \\
\textit{Usamos el Presente Simple para hablar de hábitos, rutinas y hechos.}

\bigskip
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{llll}
\bfseries\color{kidsOrange}Pronoun & \bfseries\color{kidsOrange}Verb & \bfseries Example \\[4pt]
\hline\\[-6pt]
I              & wake up  & I wake up at 7 a.m.         \\
You            & wake up  & You wake up early.          \\
He / She / It  & wake\textbf{s} up & She wake\textbf{s} up at 6. \\
We / You / They & wake up & They wake up late.          \\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\bigskip
{\color{kidsRed}\faIcon{exclamation-circle}} \textbf{Important:} Add \textbf{-s} or \textbf{-es} to the verb when the subject is \textbf{He, She,} or \textbf{It}! \\
\textit{¡Agrega \textbf{-s} o \textbf{-es} al verbo cuando el sujeto es \textbf{He, She} o \textbf{It}!}
\end{grammartip}

\vspace{14pt}

% ---- Grammar Activity 1 ----
\begin{activity}{Activity 1 — Choose the correct form · Elige la forma correcta}
Circle the correct verb form. / Encierra en un círculo la forma correcta del verbo.

\medskip
\begin{enumerate}
  \item I \textbf{(wake / wakes)} up at 7 a.m. every day.
  \item She \textbf{(brush / brushes)} her teeth after breakfast.
  \item They \textbf{(go / goes)} to school by bus.
  \item He \textbf{(do / does)} his homework in the afternoon.
  \item We \textbf{(have / has)} dinner at 7 p.m.
  \item My dog \textbf{(sleep / sleeps)} all day!
\end{enumerate}
\end{activity}

% ---- Negatives tip ----
\begin{grammartip}{Negative Form · Forma negativa}
To make a negative sentence, add \textbf{don't} (or \textbf{doesn't} for He/She/It) before the verb:

\medskip
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{ll}
I \textbf{don't} watch TV in the morning. & She \textbf{doesn't} eat lunch at home. \\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\end{grammartip}
