\begin{readingbox}{Amazing Animals of the World}
Our planet is home to millions of incredible animals. Let's learn about three of them!

\medskip
\textbf{The Cheetah}

The cheetah is the fastest land animal in the world. It can run up to 120 kilometres per hour! Cheetahs live in Africa. They have yellow fur with black spots. Cheetahs eat other animals like gazelles and rabbits. Unfortunately, cheetahs are \textit{endangered} --- there are very few left in the wild.

\medskip
\textbf{The Dolphin}

Dolphins are very intelligent animals. They live in the ocean and can communicate with each other using sounds. Dolphins are not fish --- they are \textit{mammals}, which means they breathe air. They can jump very high and love to play! Dolphins eat fish and squid.

\medskip
\textbf{The Eagle}

Eagles are large birds that can fly very high in the sky. They have excellent eyesight and can see small animals from far away. Eagles build their nests in tall trees or on cliffs. They eat fish, rabbits, and other small animals. In many countries, the eagle is a symbol of freedom and power.
\end{readingbox}
