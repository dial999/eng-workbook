\section{Let's Write! / ¡Vamos a escribir!}

% ---- Activity 1 ----
\begin{activity}{Activity 1 — Complete the dialogue · Completa el diálogo}
Read and fill in the blanks using the words from the box.
\medskip

\begin{center}
\fbox{\parbox{0.8\linewidth}{%
\centering
\color{kidsBlue}\bfseries
years old \quad from \quad name \quad like \quad fine
}}
\end{center}

\medskip
\begin{tabular}{ll}
Alex: & Hi! My \answerline[3cm] is Alex. What's your name?     \\[6pt]
Sam:  & Hello! I'm Sam. How are you?                             \\[6pt]
Alex: & I'm \answerline[2.5cm], thank you! I am 11 \answerline[2.5cm]. \\[6pt]
Sam:  & Cool! I am \answerline[2cm] Mexico. Where are you from?   \\[6pt]
Alex: & I'm from Argentina. I \answerline[2cm] football!           \\[6pt]
Sam:  & Me too! Let's play!                                        \\
\end{tabular}
\end{activity}

% ---- Activity 2 ----
\begin{activity}{Activity 2 — Write about yourself · Escribe sobre ti}
Complete the sentences with your own information. / Completa las oraciones con tu propia información.

\medskip
\begin{enumerate}
  \item My name is \answerline[7cm].
  \item I am \answerline[3cm] years old.
  \item I am from \answerline[6cm].
  \item I live in \answerline[6cm].
  \item My favourite colour is \answerline[5cm].
  \item I like \answerline[6.5cm].
\end{enumerate}
\end{activity}

\vspace{14pt}
