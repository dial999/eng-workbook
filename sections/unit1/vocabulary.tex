\begin{grammartip}{Did You Know? · ¿Sabías que?}
In English we always write \textbf{I} (yo) with a capital letter, no matter where it appears in the sentence!

\medskip
\textit{Example:} \textbf{I} like pizza. \quad Yesterday \textbf{I} went to school.
\end{grammartip}
