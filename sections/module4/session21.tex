\section*{Session 21: Places in the City}
\textbf{Objective:} Name city places and say where you go.

\subsection*{Vocabulary}

\subsection*{Grammar}
\begin{grammartip}{Where do you go?}
  To say where you are going, use \textbf{I go to the...} (Yo voy al / a la...)\\
  \textbf{Examples:}\\
  I go to the park. (Voy al parque.)\\
  Where do you go to watch a movie? I go to the cinema. (¿A dónde vas para ver una película? Voy al cine.)
\end{grammartip}
